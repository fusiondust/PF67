How this app is meant to be used

PF67 organizes repeatable process knowledge into Protocols, then lets you start those Protocols as live Flows. The Dashboard is the attention surface: it should bring urgent, scheduled, active, and recently changed work into focus.

Protocols are the reusable instructions. Flows are the live or scheduled executions of those instructions. History contains the Calendar and Activity Log.

How this app is meant to be used

Build reusable Protocols for repeatable timed processes, then start or schedule real Flows from those protocols.

The Dashboard works like a front page: late or risky work is pulled into focus, active Flows stay visible, and recent activity remains one click away.

Completed work belongs in the Archive as a chronological project story with notes, step completions, and supporting media.
