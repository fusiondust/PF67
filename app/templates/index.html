How this app is meant to be used

PF67 organizes repeatable process knowledge into Protocols, then lets you start those Protocols as live Flows.

The Dashboard works like a front page: late or risky work is pulled into focus, active Flows stay visible, and recent activity remains one click away.

Protocols are reusable instructions. Flows are live or scheduled executions of those instructions. Completed work belongs in the Archive as a chronological project story.
